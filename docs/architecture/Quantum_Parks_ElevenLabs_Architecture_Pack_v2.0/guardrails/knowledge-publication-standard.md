# Knowledge Publication Standard

Quantum Parks is the master knowledge store. Every version has an owner, category, park/language scope, risk class, effective dates, checksum and approval state.

Publication requires:

1. Valid source and malware/file checks.
2. Extraction, sanitization and injection scanning.
3. Duplicate, conflict and stale-content checks.
4. Human review.
5. Two-person approval for high-risk content.
6. Immutable knowledge release manifest.
7. Durable provider publication.
8. Provider read-back and dependency verification.
9. Linked regression tests.
10. Activation and audit evidence.

Public webpages may be imported as drafts, but never overwrite active knowledge automatically. Call-derived gaps become drafts only after human action.
