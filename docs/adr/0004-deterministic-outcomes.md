# ADR 0004: Deterministic business outcomes

Status: Accepted · 2026-07-22

Model schemas may produce evidence-linked summaries, intent, topics, entities, sentiment indicators, quality signals, gaps, and recommendations. They reject completed-action claims. Outcomes are derived only from persisted tool results, transfer results, delivery receipts, callback/task records, policy detections, and conversation-end events. Requests, attempts, queued work, success, failure, and unconfirmed state remain distinct.
